# Toyota Camry 2024 — Engine Cooling System

## Engine Coolant Temperature Gauge

The engine coolant temperature gauge indicates the temperature of the engine
coolant. If the gauge needle enters the red zone, the engine is overheating.
A high coolant temperature can be caused by low coolant level, coolant leaks,
a failed thermostat, a blocked radiator, or a faulty cooling fan.

## Warning Lamp — High Engine Coolant Temperature

If the high engine coolant temperature warning lamp illuminates, the engine
coolant temperature is excessive. Do not continue driving with the warning
lamp on. Continuing to drive could cause severe engine damage.

### Immediate Actions

1. Pull over to a safe location and stop the vehicle.
2. Turn off the air conditioning system to reduce engine load.
3. Turn the engine off and wait for the engine to cool down.
4. Do not open the hood immediately if steam is visible around the engine.
5. Do not remove the radiator cap while the engine and radiator are hot.
   Pressurized hot coolant may spray out and cause severe burns.

## Coolant Level Check

Check the coolant level in the coolant reservoir when the engine is cool. The
level should be between the FULL and LOW marks. Top-up with the correct
Toyota Super Long Life Coolant diluted with clean water. Do not use plain
water alone.

## Cooling System Inspection Points

When the coolant temperature is high, check the following items:

- Coolant level in the reservoir (top up if between marks is low).
- Hoses and hose clamps for cracks, swelling, or coolant leakage.
- Radiator core and condenser for blockage by dirt and debris.
- Water pump area for coolant seepage.
- Cooling fan operation — the fans should cycle on as temperature rises.
- Thermostat operation — a stuck-closed thermostat causes overheating.
- Drive belt tension and condition.

## Engine Coolant Specification

For the 2024 model year, use Toyota Super Long Life Coolant (pink) or an
equivalent high-quality ethylene-glycol based non-silicate, non-amine,
non-nitrite coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology. Coolant
mixture ratio: 50/50 coolant to water. Capacity: approximately 6.4 litres.

## Warning — Cooling System Pressure

The cooling system operates under pressure. The radiator cap and the release
plug must only be opened when the engine and radiator are cool. Opening the
radiator cap while the system is hot releases pressurized coolant and steam,
which can cause burns and serious injury.
